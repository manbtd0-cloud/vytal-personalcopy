# Save a Child's Heart 25 → Vytal Reference Crosswalk

**Purpose:** Convert the forensic reference audit into an explicit, section-by-section Vytal translation.  
**Rule:** Copy the **narrative job, rhythm, density, motion family, and composition logic** wherever useful; never copy protected copy/assets or fabricate proof.

---

# 1. Translation legend

For each reference unit this document records:

- **Reference job** — what the original section/page is accomplishing.
- **Reference mechanics** — layout, imagery, motion, pacing, interaction.
- **Vytal equivalent** — the product/story idea that plays the same role.
- **Implementation stance** — preserve, reinterpret, or reject.
- **Media state** — real content, sourced content, illustrative content, or placeholder.

# 12. What the current implementation gets wrong relative to the reference

The current public site is not bad. The redesign is necessary because its **physical rhythm is too consistent**.

## 12.1 Current Home composition

Current order:

- Hero
- Camera Science
- Process
- Product Proof
- Screening Ecosystem
- Trust
- AI Explanation
- Longitudinal
- Impact
- Science Preview
- Future Vision
- Final CTA

This narrative is sensible, but almost every unit behaves like a product-section component.

### Redesign correction

Keep the ideas, change their **editorial weight and geometry**.

## 12.2 Current hero

Strengths:

- strong mystery copy;
- Vytal-specific optical motif;
- good medical disclaimer;
- no WebGL.

Weakness relative to reference:

- still looks primarily like typography over an abstract background;
- lacks human/media evidence;
- resolves into product explanation too quickly;
- feels like a premium SaaS hero rather than a cinematic documentary opener.

### Correction
Keep copy concept, replace abstract-only backdrop with production-sized hero media frame + Signal Thread.

## 12.3 Current process cards

Strength:
Clear logic.

Weakness:
Four-card structure is exactly the sort of repeated component geometry the reference avoids.

### Correction
Turn the same four concepts into four visually different editorial chapters.

## 12.4 Current screening Spotlight grid

Strength:
Clear and technically reusable.

Weakness:
Equal cards flatten differences in maturity and importance.

### Correction
Home uses marquee + selected editorial features. Full Screenings page handles structured comparison/status.

## 12.5 Current trust section

Strength:
One of the strongest Vytal ideas.

### Correction
Keep the idea but make it much more sparse and dominant—the reference-style emotional reset.

## 12.6 Current AI section

Strength:
`Measurements first. Explanation second.` is correct.

### Correction
Retain the controlled raw→explained transformation, but place it inside broader human/evidence pacing instead of letting it read as another isolated feature demo.

## 12.7 Current longitudinal section

Strength:
Excellent conceptual bridge.

Weakness:
Currently too much like a product feature/chart section.

### Correction
Tie it directly to time, context, and the Impact story system.

## 12.8 Current impact

## 12.9 Current future vision

# 13. Final crosswalk principle

The redesign is not complete if a reviewer can say:

`This is the old Vytal landing page with more photos and a marquee.`

The target is a deeper structural transformation:

- reference-like long-form pacing;
- reference-like chapter variation;
- reference-like semantic motif recurrence;
- reference-like human/media density;
- reference-like story overlays;
- reference-like timeline treatment;
- reference-like route specialization;
- Vytal-specific science, uncertainty, accessibility, and product truth.

The final site should make the reference influence obvious to someone who knows both experiences, while remaining impossible to mistake for a reskinned Save a Child's Heart site.
